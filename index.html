Your money, on your phone.

PocketLedger is a private expense tracker for Android. It works offline, keeps your records on your device, and can back them up, encrypted, to your own Google Drive.

Coming soon to Google Play Read the privacy policy

Everything you need, nothing you don't

One-tap presets

Save the things you add every month, like rent, savings or a donation, and record them with a single tap. Mistake? Undo it right away.

EMI and loans

Plan installments with a full schedule, and track money you gave or took with partial repayments and what is still outstanding.

Charity

Record Zakat, Sadaqah and other giving with recipients, and watch your progress against a monthly goal.

Clear reports

Cash flow, monthly and yearly reports with charts. Export to PDF or CSV and share them.

Eight languages

English, Bangla, Hindi, Arabic, Urdu, French, Spanish and Japanese, with right-to-left layouts and native digits.

Lock and reminders

Protect the app with a PIN or fingerprint, and get private reminders for EMIs, loans and daily entries.

Private by design

No account with us, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Your records stay in a database on your phone. If you turn on backup, an encrypted copy goes to a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only the app can open. You can delete everything at any time.
